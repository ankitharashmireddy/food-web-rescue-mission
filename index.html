About this game

Food Web Rescue Mission is a Class 12 Biology learning game about the Ecosystem chapter. It teaches ecosystem interdependence, trophic levels, unidirectional energy flow, the 10% law and trophic cascades.

The game is a static, offline-friendly website built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No server, database or external libraries are required. Progress and settings are stored in your browser using Local Storage.

Target audienceClass 12 Biology students
Core missions2 assessed + 1 energy challenge
StorageLocal Storage only
NetworkWorks offline

Instructions

How to Play

1

Learn

Use Eco-Pedia, the Learning Zone and the Explorer to understand the seven organisms and their feeding links.

2

Build

In Mission 1, click a food source and then its consumer. Correct links earn +10. Reversed or invalid links cost 3 points and show a hint.

3

Disturb

In Mission 2, remove the snake, predict the population changes, then run the simulation to check your answer. A correct prediction earns +30.

4

Master

In Mission 3, calculate energy transfer with the 10% law and answer three quick questions. Finish all missions to become an Ecosystem Guardian.

Golden rule: arrows always point from the food source to the organism that eats it — the direction energy travels.
