How to Play

Move your Eco Guardian with WASD or arrow keys. Walk near glowing organisms and press E to discover them.

Collect organisms, open the Eco Journal, rebuild the food chain, solve the energy puzzle, and restore ecosystem stability.

Arrows show energy flow from food to consumer. About 10% of energy moves to the next trophic level.
